03How to Request a Refund

  • Email mssvpn@gmail.com within 7 days of activation (or, for a delayed delivery, any time before activation).
  • Include: your name, the transaction ID or receipt (from CHIP/FPX/DuitNow), the service involved, and the reason for the request.
  • We respond with a decision within 5 business days.
  • For custom development projects, refund requests are accepted within 30 days of the missed milestone deadline.

04How Refunds Are Paid

Approved refunds are issued through CHIP to your original payment method (your FPX online banking account or DuitNow-linked account), or by bank transfer to an account in the payer's name where a refund to the original method is not possible. Depending on your bank, funds normally appear within 5–14 business days after we submit the refund.

05Duplicate and Erroneous Payments

If you are charged twice for the same period, or charged the wrong amount, we will refund the duplicate/extra amount in full once verified, regardless of the windows above. Please report it as soon as you notice it.

06Non-Refundable Items

  • Completed development milestones and delivered custom work.
  • Third-party costs already incurred on your behalf (domain registration, paid licences, third-party API fees).
  • Service periods already used beyond the 7-day window.
  • Accounts terminated for abuse, fraud, or unlawful use of the service.
  • Voluntary contributions sent via the donation QR on our website — these are gifts, not purchases, and are non-refundable.

07Service Credits Instead of Refunds

For significant downtime caused by us, we first offer service credits (extension of your subscription period). A cash refund applies only if the fault persists and we cannot restore the service.

08Before You Dispute

If something is wrong with a payment, please contact us first. Payment disputes raised directly with your bank take longer for everyone and may result in service suspension while the case is open. We resolve genuine issues quickly through the process above.

09Renewals and Cancellation

Subscriptions are renewed by manual payment — we do not store any payment instrument and there is no auto-debit. To cancel a renewal, email us before your renewal date; the service remains active until the end of the period you have already paid for.
